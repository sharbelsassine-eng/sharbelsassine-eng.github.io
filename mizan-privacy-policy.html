Mizan Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Mizan is a money tracker for Android, made by Sharbel Sassine, Haifa, Israel. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Your records stay on your device

Everything you enter in Mizan, including transactions, accounts and balances, categories, budgets, repeating payments and notes, is stored only on your phone. Mizan does not send your records to the developer or to any server, and the developer cannot see them.

  • Imports: a CSV or backup file you choose to import is read on your phone and is not sent anywhere.
  • Automatic backup: once a day Mizan saves a copy of your records as a file in the Downloads/Mizan folder on your phone, so you can restore them after reinstalling the app. The file stays on your phone and is not sent anywhere, but other apps you have allowed to read your files can open it. You can turn this off in Settings, under Automatic daily backup.
  • Exports and backups: files you export or back up are saved where you choose. What happens to them after that is up to you.
  • Home-screen widgets: the widgets show figures stored on your phone.
  • App lock: if you set a PIN, only a one-way hash of it is stored on your phone. If you unlock with your fingerprint, Android handles the check and Mizan never receives your fingerprint data.
  • Android backup: if Android's own backup is turned on, Android may include Mizan's data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Google under its terms, and the developer has no access to it.

Advertising

Mizan is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner at the top of the screen, and occasionally a full-screen ad after you save a new entry. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as the phone model, operating system and app version
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information about ad performance

Google uses this information to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise ads. None of your financial records are passed to the ads system. To learn more, see the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Mizan asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your phone's settings under Google, then Ads, or Privacy, then Ads, depending on your Android version.
  • Deleting your data: Settings, then Erase all data removes everything Mizan stores on your phone. Uninstalling the app also removes it, except the automatic backup files in Downloads/Mizan, which you can delete yourself.

Information the developer collects

None. The developer runs no server for Mizan and does not collect, store or sell personal information. Information processed for advertising is handled by Google, as described above.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob, as described above.
  • Biometric: used only if you turn on fingerprint unlock.
  • Vibration: used for light feedback when you tap.
  • Basic phone state, wake lock and foreground service: added by Google's ad library for its own use. Mizan itself does not use them.

Children

Mizan is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
