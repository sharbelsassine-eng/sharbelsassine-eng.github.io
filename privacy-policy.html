7z Opener Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

7z Opener ("the app") is developed by Sharbel Sassine. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Your files stay on your device

The app opens, lists and extracts archives only on your device. It does not upload your archives, the files inside them, their names or your passwords anywhere, and the developer has no access to them. Archive passwords are kept in memory only while the archive is open and are never saved. Temporary copies of files you open from an archive are kept in the app's private storage and deleted the next time the app starts. The app reaches your files only through the Android file and folder pickers, and only the files and folders you choose.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app automatically collects the following and shares it with Google: your device's IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location), your device's advertising ID and app set ID, interactions with the app and with ads (such as app launches, taps and ad views), and diagnostic information about the app's performance. This data is encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice at any time under About, then Ad privacy settings. In every region you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings, which limits personalized advertising.

Information the developer collects

None. The developer does not run any server for the app and does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The app has no accounts and no analytics of its own. Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to delete on the developer's side. For data held by Google, use the controls in your Google account and device settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
